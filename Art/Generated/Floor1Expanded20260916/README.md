# Floor 1 expanded world — art milestones

The current independent art sample includes 12 interactive modular houses, 32 exterior LOD houses and three furnished ground floors. Its approved northwest ten-tree native-oak pocket is default-on, with `--north-grove-trial=off` retaining the earlier one-tree view. The [60-second v3 camera tour](../../../exports/floor1-art-20260916/floor1-world-tour-v3.mp4) and [v3 provenance](milestone-v3-provenance.json) preserve 11 decoded video frames, source/asset hashes, 254/254 default and 249/249 fallback world checks, and exited rendering/encoding processes. The earlier [v2 movie](../../../exports/floor1-art-20260916/floor1-world-tour-v2.mp4) and [v1 movie](../../../exports/floor1-art-20260916/floor1-world-tour-v1.mp4) are retained byte unchanged. The v3 route is a continuous camera tour through the real scene, not a recording of player walking; offline fixed 30fps is only visual evidence. The northeastern land perimeter, sparse country outside the pocket and distant visual-only labyrinth remain unfinished.

This dedicated, independent Godot art scene lays out a continuous 420 × 460 m ground with a 180 × 170 m town, an 8 m market street, a 24 m plaza and fountain, two 5 m residential lanes, the selected original gatehouse, four fields, clustered low-poly woodland and a countryside path to z = −230. The [layout manifest](manifest.json) gives the exact position and orientation of 12 interactive modular dwellings and 32 exterior Meshy LOD buildings. It does not replace the canonical first-floor scene or create residents or saves.

The [milestone video](../../../exports/floor1-art-20260916/floor1-world-tour-v1.mp4) is a real, continuously interpolated camera route in this 3D scene through the plaza, street, homes, gate and countryside. Its 901 fixed offline frames produce a 30.03 s native 1400 × 900 H.264 MP4. It is visual evidence, not a runtime frame-rate measurement. The original AVI, route data and seven extracted MP4 frames are recorded in the [provenance receipt](milestone-v1-provenance.json), which also freezes the scene source and dependent model hashes. The [eight corrected GPU stills](milestone-v1-stills/) are actual 1680 × 1080 PNGs; earlier review sets remain in `tmp/floor1-art-20260916/world/captures-v1/`, `captures-v2-v3/` and `captures-v3-v4/`.

The first-pass independent world test passed 144/144 actual Godot checks. It covers the ground and path, gate clearance, building footprints, fountain and benches, and every modular house's lane-to-interior open route and closed door with the **actual walker capsule** (0.32 m radius, 1.80 m height). F selects the nearest actual front, rear or side window opening and toggles all windows of that house; E toggles its door. No per-window control is claimed. See [first-pass physics evidence](../../../tmp/floor1-art-20260916/world/physics.json).

The final eight non-headless Vulkan Forward+ stills each sampled 120 post-draw intervals after warm-up with VSync on. The market view measured 4.166 ms median, 4.289 ms p95 and 3,524 draw calls including shadows at the saved image size. The panorama p95 was 5.198 ms. These are presentation-inclusive frame intervals, not pure GPU time or a promise of performance on other hardware. [Capture JSON](milestone-v1-stills/capture.json) records all views and primitive counts.

The selected [fountain](../../../game/assets/floor1/plaza_fountain_20260916/manifest.json) remains an unmodified source GLB, uniformly normalized to 2.2 m. Exterior Meshy houses are visual shells; door and window behavior belongs to modular dwellings. The distant labyrinth entry is a grounded **visual-only unfinished silhouette**. Courtyard detail, countryside vegetation and the sparse outer town still need a further coherent art pass.

A [versioned second-pass layout](manifest-v5-yards.json) now groups maple/planter courts and six roadside forest pockets, with 133 measured tree and 334 vegetation instances. Its own 153/153 physics checks and eight actual optimized-v5 GPU stills are saved separately from the frozen first video. The residence and country foregrounds read more clearly, while the flat pale ground and distant maze still need art work. Dedicated furnished-ground-floor inserts are being authored separately and are not in this second-pass still set.

The procedural ground's pale value was traced to a rendering color-space error: raw sRGB hex components were fed to a linear shader albedo. The [same-view actual GPU A/B and numeric pixel report](../../../tmp/floor1-art-20260916/world/shader-color-ab.json) preserves the eight pre-fix stills and the eight corrected stills, with the original hex palette, geometry, sun and fog fixed. Country grass changed from RGB (183,198,169) to (119,150,105) at a measured foreground pixel; sampled imported oak canopy and sky pixels were identical. The correction uses one `Color.srgb_to_linear()` conversion as described by [Godot's shader color-space guidance](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/shaders/shader_reference/shading_language.html#using-source-color). The broad ground remains geometrically flat and the maze entry remains unfinished.

Launch the dedicated walkable scene from the project root with:

```powershell
& 'D:/lucidgloves/InfiniteAincrad/tmp/toolchain/Godot_v4.7.2-stable_mono_win64/Godot_v4.7.2-stable_mono_win64.exe' --path 'D:/lucidgloves/InfiniteAincrad/game' --scene res://scenes/floor1_expanded_world.tscn
```

WASD walks, Shift runs, E opens/closes a nearby door, F toggles the selected house's windows, and Esc releases the mouse. This scene does not run the town kernel or save world state.
The current graphical capture used this local executable/project/scene with Godot 4.7.2 Forward+ on Vulkan 1.4.325 (RTX 5080). This plain command itself was not separately hand-operated in the v3 batch.
