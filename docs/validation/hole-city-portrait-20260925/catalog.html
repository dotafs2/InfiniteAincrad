ORIGINAL BLENDER ASSETS · PORTRAIT GODOT BUILD

A city with 208 models.

48 buildings · 32 vehicles · 64 street props · 48 plants · 16 stick figures.
Every asset is used in the playable city. Related variants change geometry; recolors and repeated instances are not counted as new models.

Characters include Idle, Walk, Run and one unique role action each (Chase, Sneak, Patrol, Eat Fries, Hose, Flip, Skate, Shoot, Jog, Strum, Hammer, Care, Pedal, Hero Pose, Carry or Dance). Contact sheets show rest poses. The native game uses a 540 × 960 portrait view and one-finger dragging. This remains a first-level reference reconstruction, with further map, shop, skin and art fidelity work pending.
