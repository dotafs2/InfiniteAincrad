# Aincrad world charter v1

InfiniteAincrad uses the original SAO **Aincrad** setting as its reference. A GM may
extend life inside that setting; it may not silently replace its rules with those
of another game. This document is a project constraint, not an assertion that
every planned system already exists or that our original villagers are canon.

## Setting and scope

- Aincrad is a hundred-floor world. Floor progression and access must become
  explicit world state before content depends on them. The present town is a
  prototype; a completed hundred-floor game is not implemented.
- Equipment, crafting and scarce materials belong to the setting. A profession
  title or character description does not grant a skill, item or resource.
- Town safety and wilderness danger must be explicit rules when combat arrives.
  Do not claim the present non-combat town implements SAO's complete safe-zone,
  duel, crime, death or revival systems. Any such addition needs a separate review.
- Keep sword skills, equipment progression and the Col economy as design
  references. Do not import D&D spell slots/classes/races/alignment mechanics,
  ALO flight or GGO firearms as ordinary Aincrad abilities. Names or text alone
  are not sufficient evidence that a mechanic is or is not compatible.
- Original persistent villagers, rich personalities, voluntary cooperation and
  background developer GMs are **project extensions**. GMs are infrastructure,
  not villagers, divine in-world speakers or a claim to reproduce Cardinal.
- Ordinary travel uses physical bodies and fixed navigation algorithms. A model
  selects a known goal or permitted action, not a succession of steering vectors.
  A future setting-appropriate teleport item would need its own finite costs,
  access checks and capability; it must never hide a failed walking journey.

## Invariants for every GM proposal

1. `setting`: state whether the change is a supported setting adaptation,
   a compatible original extension, or engine maintenance. Cite the basis and
   explain the compatibility. Uncertain canon stays uncertain until reviewed.
2. `capabilities`: use the versioned common action boundary for resident behavior,
   observable receipts and actual prerequisite/resource checks. A configuration
   or narrative description alone does not establish a working ability.
3. `agency`: residents may accept, refuse or ignore opportunities. Access to
   another person's property or joint action requires the appropriate consent.
4. `knowledge`: only attributed perception, disclosure and actual history can
   teach a resident facts. GM evidence does not become resident omniscience.
5. `continuity`: preserve stable identities, complete sequences, commitments,
   inventory and unfinished jobs through save/load. Never reset to conceal failure.
6. `execution`: fixed navigation, physics and world reducers decide outcomes.
   No model teleportation, fabricated arrival, free material, retroactive action
   or manufactured adoption. Unknown costs remain unresolved; episodes are bounded.

## Release and continuing evolution

Production autonomy must pin this charter and the action capability standard by
content hash. Each coding scope carries a compatibility rationale acknowledging
all six invariants. The host rejects absent/stale pins, missing declarations or
attempts to change the pinned design from a GM candidate. These are structural
checks; they cannot prove the semantic truth of a model's rationale.

The main review still examines concrete behavior and tests, including migration,
physical travel where applicable, and effects in the same world. A concrete
setting violation is a release blocker. Original GM follow-up must distinguish
installed, offered, accepted, completed and still unused. No change is valid.
Repeated bounded cycles, persisted GM memory and explicit recovery are the route
to continuing development. They do not promise infinite uptime or unlimited cost.

## Reference sources

- [Licensed first Aincrad novel, Yen Press](https://yenpress.com/titles/9780316560856-sword-art-online-1-aincrad-light-novel): the hundred-floor premise.
- [Official anime, episode 2](https://www.swordart-online.net/aincrad/story/02.html): the first-floor boss and coordinated progression.
- [Official anime, episode 5](https://www.swordart-online.net/aincrad/story/05.html): protected towns and the distinction between ordinary safety and the investigated incident.
- [Official anime, episode 7](https://www.swordart-online.net/aincrad/story/07.html): smithing, weapon durability and rare crafting metal.

References establish the broad setting, not implementation formulas. Proposed
combat, sword-skill, currency, death and teleport rules need their own primary
source review before implementation. Other licensed game adaptations are not
automatically evidence for the original Aincrad rules.
