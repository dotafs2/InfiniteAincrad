Description:

Guide a hungry black hole, swallow what fits, and grow into your next feast.
Every round lasts one minute, with bigger targets waiting as your hole expands.

FEAST ON TWO WORLDS
Snack Garden is an edible city of donuts, pancakes, burgers, cakes and sushi.
Start with little bites and work toward towering stacks. In Sunny City, devour
street objects, vehicles and larger city targets as you grow.

CHASE THE SUSHI EXPRESS
Find the moving sushi train and swallow its separate cars when your hole is
large enough. Sunny City has its own City Tram to chase.

GROW AND BOOST
Earn coins through ordinary play, compete with computer-controlled rival holes,
and use BOOST for a short burst of speed.

FIND YOUR LOOK
Browse 44 original hole skins in one collection, ordered from Common to
Prismatic. Skin unlocks use in-game coins, with draw rates and guarantees
shown in the collection.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Adjust music, sound effects and light haptics. Local progress keeps your coins
and collection on your device. The iOS version includes optional rewarded
videos and Apple coin packs when available; neither is needed to play.
