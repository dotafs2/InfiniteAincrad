Description:

Guide a hungry black hole, swallow what fits, and grow toward bigger targets.
Choose your world on HOME before each round: Sunny City, Snack Garden or
Bubble Toy Town. Start small and find your next mouthful.

THREE WORLDS, THREE FLAVORS
Sunny City brings busy streets, vehicles and towering city targets.
Snack Garden is an edible city of donuts, pancakes, burgers, cakes and sushi.
Bubble Toy Town has pillowy castles, rounded towers, toy vehicles and a lively
royal court. Find marching guards, couriers, jesters and other palace folk.

CHASE THE SUSHI EXPRESS
Track the moving sushi train and swallow its separate cars when your hole is
large enough. Sunny City has its own City Tram to chase.

SMALL BITES, BIG APPETITE
Earn points, grow your hole, compete with computer-controlled rivals and use
BOOST for a short burst of speed. Watch real objects tumble into the hole.

ONE MORE MOUTHFUL
Sunny City and Bubble Toy Town start with 90 seconds; Snack Garden starts with
60 seconds. A timed finish can offer one extra 30 seconds in the same round.
Use five diamonds or an available optional rewarded video. Earned round coins
can also receive one 1.5x boost using one diamond or an available rewarded video.
These two choices are independent, and each can be used once per round.

FIND YOUR LOOK
Browse 44 original hole skins in one scrolling collection, from Common to
Prismatic. Unlocks use in-game coins; RATES shows the current probabilities and
guarantees. Exchange diamonds for coins, earn diamonds from available optional
videos, or buy optional diamond packs through Apple. Purchases and videos are
not required to play.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Adjust music, sound and haptics in Settings. Progress and preferences stay on
your device. No game sign-in or cloud account is required. Core gameplay works
offline; ads and Apple purchases require their services to be available.

## Review-note facts for the subsequent build

Verify the final native version/build before using these notes. The accepted
0.13.0 game contains Bubble Toy Town; the toy map remains absent from rejected
0.12.7/build26. Mac package acceptance is not native installation or purchase proof.

No game sign-in is required. HOME offers the three playable maps. Select a map,
press PLAY and drag to steer. Changing worlds happens on HOME between rounds,
not by teleporting during a round. Rivals are computer-controlled.

The bottom navigation is HOME, STORE, HOLES. STORE contains the optional
three-diamond rewarded video and diamond-to-coin exchanges. DIAMOND SHOP opens
the consumable 60 / 360 / 1,800 diamond products. Runtime prices come from
StoreKit; missing catalog entries remain unavailable. Apple verifies purchases,
and the wallet saves the transaction binding before acknowledgment.

A result can offer 1.5x earned coins for one diamond or a rewarded video.
A timed result can also offer one 30-second continuation for five diamonds or
a rewarded video. Both offers are independent. Video coin boosts and extensions
require the accepted earned event and actual ad dismissal. A store diamond
video credits on the accepted earned event; repeat actions remain disabled
until dismissal. The provider determines video length. Merely closing an
unfinished video gives no reward.

HOME > SETTINGS contains Music, Sound, Haptics and PRIVACY & SUPPORT. The latter
contains Privacy, Support and Credits. AD PRIVACY CHOICES is shown when Google
UMP requires it. The iOS app uses AdMob/UMP and StoreKit 2; non-personalized ads
still involve provider data processing. Do not claim zero data collection.
