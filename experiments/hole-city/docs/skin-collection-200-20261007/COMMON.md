# Common tier: twenty authored skins

Status: twenty concepts and twenty local runtime models completed, October 7,
2026. Independent review accepted the final isolated GPU appearance after
rejecting earlier candidates. White describes the rarity badge; body colors
follow the material and theme. Rows 1–6 preserve existing Common identities;
rows 7–20 append fourteen new identities. Prices and owned-skin migration remain.

The table preserves the original motion targets. Current runtime feedback uses
one short whole-model bounce plus seven themed pooled particle profiles, with
two particles for an item and eight for a rival. It does not claim independently
articulated fingers, handles, staves or knots. The actual game meshes are
performance-conscious interpretations, not the full detail of the concept art.
See [current verification and playable delivery](CONTINUE.md).

All forms frame an unobstructed gameplay opening. Silhouette and material regions
must identify the theme at the normal camera scale. Every skin has a small item
response and a stronger brief rival response; neither changes swallowing speed.
The motions and particles below are cosmetic, bounded and pooled. None requires
continuous fluid, cloth, rigid-body-fragment or foliage simulation.

## Review notes

- Slate Ring keeps its legacy `cocoa_square` ID and starter ownership. Its current
  displayed name is Slate Ring; it is not presented as a new chocolate skin.
- Shell Cup is a small solid shell object, distinct from the existing coral
  assembly and living jellyfish. Enamel Basin is solid household enamel with a
  brief droplet cue; it does not duplicate Rainfall's clouds or animated pool.
- Woven Nest has no owl, eggs or character animation; Leaf Cup has one authored
  leaf rather than the higher-tier plant system. These keep the Common tier clear.
- Pancake Stack retains broad smooth layers, distinct from the higher-tier
  gridded Waffle Stack. Catcher's Mitt is a single low form, without a character
  or ongoing articulated hand simulation; use three main padded forms rather
  than articulated fingers. Rope Coil, Woven Nest and Knit Cap use baked weave
  detail and a few large strands instead of individually modeled fibers.
- Independent review replaces flat Denim Patch with the shaped Denim Pocket and
  requires different container silhouettes: banded staves, an asymmetric pot lip,
  a bent tin handle and a wide shallow basin flange.
- All twenty now have distinct cached meshes and materials. Final isolated
  captures and the contact sheet show actual game models. Ordinary gameplay
  evidence and package acceptance are recorded separately in the handoff.
