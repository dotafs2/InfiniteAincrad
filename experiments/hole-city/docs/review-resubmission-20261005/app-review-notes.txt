2. Purpose/audience: A single-player casual arcade game about collecting and growing a black hole, with computer-controlled rivals. Players freely select Sunny City, Snack Garden or Bubble Toy Town from HOME between rounds. All three worlds are available without a world-unlock purchase. Snack Garden includes the Sushi Express train. Skins and progress are saved locally.

3. Setup/features: No app account, credentials or sample files are required. HOME > select world > PLAY. Drag to move; BOOST gives a short speed increase when charged. City and Toy Town start at 90 seconds; Snack Garden at 60. HOLES opens the skin collection. SETTINGS > PRIVACY & SUPPORT opens support, privacy and credits.

4. Services: Core gameplay runs locally without a developer-operated game backend, cloud saves or AI service. Optional rewarded ads use Google AdMob and applicable privacy forms use Google UMP. Purchases use Apple StoreKit 2. Ads request non-personalized delivery; publisher first-party ID/personalization are disabled. SDK data processing is disclosed in the privacy policy and App Privacy information. Network/provider availability affects ads and purchases.
