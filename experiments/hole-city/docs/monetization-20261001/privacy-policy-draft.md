Local progress
Coins, owned skins, selected maps, audio preferences and redeemed transaction IDs are saved locally. We do not receive these saves or provide accounts or cloud saves. Operating-system backups are managed by Apple. Removing the app may remove this progress.

Rewarded advertising on iOS
The iOS app uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to provide optional rewarded videos and manage privacy choices. Google may process IP addresses and approximate location, device identifiers, ad interactions, app interactions and diagnostic/performance information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. We request non-personalized ads and disable Google's publisher first-party ID. The app does not request Apple's tracking authorization or provide your local wallet or skin collection to Google. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing. After consent permits ad requests, the app may preload a rewarded video before you choose to watch it; skipping a video does not by itself disable the advertising SDK.

We check Google's consent status before requesting ads. Where required, Google's privacy form is presented. When Google requires a privacy-choice entry, it is available below in this Privacy page. Rewarded videos are optional. Closing a video before its earned-reward callback does not earn a bonus.

Apple purchases on iOS
Apple handles optional coin purchases. The app receives Apple-verified product and transaction information to deliver coins and prevent duplicate grants. We do not receive payment-card details, your Apple account credentials or Apple's billing address. Coin packs are consumable items. Completed purchases and spent coins are not a cloud backup and cannot be restored by us after local data is lost. Contact Apple about billing or refunds, and contact us about missing coins.

Optional reports and support
Testing reports are created locally only in testing mode and are not automatically uploaded. If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them for support and retain them only as needed. You may request deletion of support correspondence.

Your choices
You can play without buying coins or watching a rewarded video. Music and SFX can be changed in the game. Light haptics have a separate pause-menu switch. This app does not request camera, microphone, photo-library or precise-location access. Google manages retention and privacy choices for data it processes; Apple manages its platform and payment data.
