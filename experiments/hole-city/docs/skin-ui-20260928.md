# Distinct skins and casual UI — September 28, 2026

Version 0.5.0 removes the shared orbiting-sphere effect from all ten skins. Water,
accretion discs, flame ribbons, aurora curtains, leaves, crystals, mechanical
segments, stone and lanterns now use different geometry and motion. The same
builder feeds the collection preview and the equipped player.

## Reference study and source boundary

- [Chris Wallis: Making of Moana (the ShaderToy)](https://wallisc.github.io/rendering/2020/12/08/Making-Of-Moana-the-shadertoy.html):
  water reflection, absorption, refraction, caustics and white water provide the
  visual study. Tide uses original angular waves, blue-to-turquoise shading,
  moving highlights and foam, with three curling crest meshes. It does not
  implement that demo's volumetric transport or copy its code.
- [Gargantua With HDR Bloom by sonicether](https://www.shadertoy.com/view/lstSRS):
  an accretion-disc reference identified through its publicly attributed port.
  Direct ShaderToy retrieval failed. The port declares CC BY-NC-SA; its shader
  was not incorporated. The delivered shader is a new raster approximation.
- [Eric Bruneton: A Real-time High-quality Black Hole Shader](https://ebruneton.github.io/black_hole_shader/):
  reference for the disc, dark opening and lensing silhouette. Our thin meshes
  approximate the appearance; they do not simulate curved light rays.
- [Hole.io official Google Play page](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.voodoo.holeio)
  and the earlier direct [Poki observation ledger](../fidelity/REFERENCE.md):
  a strong PLAY action, a floating city miniature, clear level/time/score
  hierarchy and Classic/Special skin grids. Search results also show several
  UI generations; this pass follows the earlier observed miniature-and-PLAY
  layout instead of claiming pixel identity with every version.

No ShaderToy source or Hole.io artwork was copied into the game. Reference
pages are study sources; all five shipped effect shaders and UI graphics are
project-authored. The existing MIT template and OFL font notices still apply.

The home screen uses a floating miniature, orange PLAY, map selection and a
compact Maps/Skins dock. Skin cards contain real one-frame model thumbnails,
with one larger animated preview. HUD panels are smaller: a pill timer, score
bar, circular boost and functional skill buttons. Cosmetic skins omit the
inactive skill button. World labels read LVL and growth, replacing SECTOR/SYNC.
Skin IDs, score unlock thresholds, saved progress and skill rules are retained.

## Rendering budget and physical boundary

- Four to fifteen mesh nodes per player skin, including the normally hidden
  activation ring; at most 2,976 triangles in the structural audit.
- No common particle system and no sphere meshes. Only Cinder and Lantern
  allocate sparks, with ten and five flat quads respectively.
- No screen-color/depth copies, texture samples, full-screen effects or ray
  marching in the new shaders. Opponents retain their inexpensive basic rims.
- Thumbnail viewports render once. The large preview stops processing when
  hidden. Shader time follows the visual node, so pause stops the animation.
- The gameplay visual has no opaque mouth and owns no collision body. The
  minimum main-surface radius stays outside 0.985 of the physical opening.
  Vertical decoration growth is capped while horizontal size follows the hole.

## Verification and limits

[Artifacts](../artifacts/skin-ui/README.md) record source/PCK tests, the exported
package hashes and the shader-equation probe. Source and packaged checks include
all existing independent-object, lamp/sign and full-city swallowing suites,
plus skin structure, navigation and actual skill behavior. Portrait touch,
pause and locomotion regression checks also run.

The windowless CGL helper compiles all five shipped shader programs and renders
all ten real mesh/material sets at two shader times. Water, accretion, flames
and aurora produce changed rendered pixels. Local leaf/stone/spark movement is
checked separately by Godot. The images are **isolated shader/mesh previews**,
not Godot screenshots: the helper does not reproduce Godot color conversion,
transparent sorting, billboard handling or the complete scene renderer.

The saved browser-origin denial was not bypassed. Browser access was also
unavailable when trying to open public references this turn. No foreground
window or desktop focus change was used. Actual browser UI review and sustained
iPhone 14 60 FPS remain unverified.
