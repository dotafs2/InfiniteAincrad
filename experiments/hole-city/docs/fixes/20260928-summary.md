Review caught and returned several concrete failures: stale off-page skin
details after a swipe, inverted cap faces on 18 motifs, a splash below the
floor, visible-frustum retirement risk, missing release-input coverage,
unbounded recorder interruption metadata and release errors that could still
print a passing marker. These were fixed before final acceptance.

The stricter package isolation also caught four review scripts that wrote
reports into `res://artifacts` after their assertions had passed. Those writes
depended on the source checkout being present. Their report output is now
explicit and checked; the source and isolated-package suites must both finish
without engine errors. Passing assertions alone do not permit publication.

## Verification scope

Version 0.8.0 is installed in the local preview. All 33 source suites and all
33 truly isolated exported-PCK suites pass **10,479 checks each**. The added
independent onboarding/economy review separately passes 183 source and 183
final-package checks; these are not included in the 33-suite totals.

The [release manifest](../../artifacts/audit-fixes-20260928/release/release.json)
records 446 guarded inputs. The separate
[delivery verification](../../artifacts/audit-fixes-20260928/verification.json)
checks completion markers against reports and verifies all ten actual HTTP
artifacts from `http://127.0.0.1:8768/`, their exact bytes, isolation headers,
no-store caching and device-recorder build metadata. The final PCK SHA-256 is
`70d5ae6efbc6520a9b910e61ed4855c2bcc42287c6cb9da1e31cac55cfa29323`.

The normal food autoplay completed contested and solo rounds without injected
score. The solo run took 69.85 simulated seconds and consumed 14 buildings,
two large heaps and one landmark. This is a reproducible route test, not a
claim about the pace or success rate of human players. The full-city physical
sweep independently exercised 608 non-building objects and 48 building shells.

Three isolated CPU runs per opening/grown scenario provide 7,200 measured
frames: city P95 7.004/6.866 ms and food P95 5.043/6.835 ms, with no measured
interval above 16.67 ms. These are Mac headless CPU wall times; they exclude
GPU rendering and do not establish iPhone 14 FPS. Exact source hashes, all
sampled intervals (sorted) and summaries are under
`artifacts/audit-fixes-20260928/cpu-final`. A further
600-frame trace after four minutes of food rain retains 623 rain bodies with
P95 8.119 ms, worst 11.243 ms and no interval above 16.67 ms. Total measured
frames across these final runs: 7,800. Before/after snapshots confirm all 450
profile inputs and the engine binary remained unchanged. Raw report/log hashes
bind the traces to that snapshot; independent verification recalculates their
percentiles, over-budget counts and aggregated summaries. These samples
establish distributions, not the temporal ordering of spikes.

## Remaining external acceptance

The latest authorized computer-use inventory reports that the Mac is locked
and automatic unlock could not unlock it; browser control returns
`User unavailable`. The user already permitted viewing and playtesting, so
this is an access limitation, not a denial of permission. No real browser game
capture or physical iPhone 14 performance recording was obtained. Final
rendered polish, perceived input latency and sustained thermal/GPU performance
remain open. Acceptance requires at least ten active minutes on **each map**
on the physical iPhone 14, including growth, repeated collapses and skin
effects, with device video and frame-cadence evidence. The
[device procedure](device-acceptance.md) and opt-in recorder prepare that
verification; they do not turn missing evidence into a PASS.

The 44 silhouettes are original code-authored forms. Collection capacity and
the three observed gem-to-coin offers follow the retained reference evidence.
Unknown later reference prices, level rewards and city topology are not
claimed exact; current rules are shown explicitly in the UI. New Special
forms are identified as cosmetic unless they have an actual established skill.
Human time to the first and second earned skin unlocks has not been measured
on both maps. Time to the first bite, first upgrade and first swallowed car
also remains unverified; the automated food route does not establish those
human pacing results. The opening timer, score target and blue color follow
the observed reference values, while Core Shell retains an original accretion
form rather than a verified reproduction of the reference's plain opening.

Tests use isolated `--test` state and process-owned temporary saves. The player
save, preview server, foreground applications, resident world and Git history
are not reset or published. The 2D images under `layouts` are labeled drawing
reconstructions that omit 3D views; they are not gameplay screenshots.
