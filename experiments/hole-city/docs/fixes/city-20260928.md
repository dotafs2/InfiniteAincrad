# Sunny City audit corrections

All probes use isolated `--test` processes. No player save, foreground window,
preview server or unrelated process was changed. Runtime evidence is under
`artifacts/audit-fixes-20260928/city`; exact final source hashes are included.

1. **Heading.** Walking direction now derives from velocity, not a fixed
   per-frame displacement threshold. A bounded turn blend retains at most
   15 degrees of lag. Real controller sampling at 30/60/120 Hz passes for every
   moving person, including after crowd correction. The ordinary Walk/Run
   animation speed also uses final actual motion.
2. **Crowds.** Standing people use spatially bounded, sidewalk-constrained
   separation with a 0.48 m target for the 0.46 m physical footprint. Claimed
   and falling residents leave that controller before another scripted step.
   A 60-second 60 Hz pursuit/panic/wandering run has no footprint overlaps or
   sidewalk escapes. Eight coincident people at a path boundary resolve
   without preventing swallow release.
3. **Traffic.** Eight vehicles circulate two opposite rounded avenue loops.
   Wrapping route distance preserves position and tangent; world coordinates
   never jump to another endpoint. Actual oriented footprint separation avoids
   the false deadlock caused by overlapping axis-aligned boxes during turns.
   Over 180 seconds at 60 Hz, all cars travel 432–504 m and cross 2–3 loop seams;
   maximum displacement is 0.04667 m, with no jumps, off-road centers, incorrect
   headings or oriented footprint overlaps. Car swallowing remains unchanged.
4. **District composition.** Garden Market, Civic Quarter, North Commons and
   Canal Yards retain all 48 building models and all 208 catalog assets. The
   repeated six-row placement becomes courtyard housing, avenue shop fronts,
   a civic commons and canal-side warehouses/hotels. Each district has its own
   actual landmark and planted park. Four walk loops of three block areas
   circulate around corners. Their geometry is painted on the same cuttable
   terrain material used by the physical ground, so there is no opaque sidewalk
   or turf overlay spanning an open hole. Props and trees are kept clear of the
   walking paths, and all individual street assets are retained.
