## F1: sustained food rain

Rain still drops all ten recipes within 3.5–16 metres of the player. It does not move showers to hidden districts to satisfy the test.

The cap is 24 actually airborne pieces and 640 retained pieces, enough for an entire idle four-minute round at six pieces every 2.4 seconds. Existing visible or nearby food never expires. Replenishment removes only settled, unclaimed food at least twelve seconds old, more than 34 metres from the player, and outside the camera's expanded food envelope. Nearby food remains independently edible.

Sleeping rain shares cell/recipe MultiMeshes. A wake, swallow or distant retirement removes the exact slot through the existing compact-and-expose path. ## F2: collection-based city completion API

`record_collection(item, hole)` counts only the player's actual collection receipts and deduplicates body IDs. A building or large heap counts when at least 60% of its original independent pieces have been collected. Starter cascades do not count as large heaps. Landmark progress derives from a completed landmark building, and toppers have the same building metadata as the lower floors.

`objective_target(hole)` finds a remaining edible root or fallen piece. Once the building target is reached it stops prioritizing ordinary buildings over missing landmarks/heaps. Parent-owned game/UI changes require twelve buildings, one landmark and one large heap in addition to the original food order, expose those objectives, and allow already released bodies to settle after the round.

A normal contested autoplay completed at 64.65 simulated seconds with 1,190 points, eighteen buildings, one heap and one landmark. Solo autoplay completed at 69.85 seconds with 1,182 points, fourteen buildings, two heaps and one landmark. These runs inject no points or collections. They establish the goal is reachable in the normal simulation, not a human completion-time promise.

Independent review of the combined objective/settlement unit: **passed**, `docs/fixes/reviews/food-goals.md`.
