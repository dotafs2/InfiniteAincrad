# Independent objects and scene QA

The reported blue cluster is the Transit Exchange: a luggage carousel, bicycle
dock, ticket machine, rain shelter and route globe. The district was not one
physics body. Each recipe, however, used a single union box, so its loose
luggage and rack plates could not be swallowed separately. The old builder
also read full sphere/cylinder diameters as radii and degrees as radians.

The original level-two probe collected nine complete transit fixtures, while
the oversized route globe remained frozen. That baseline is preserved in
`artifacts/scene-qa/baseline-transit.json`. It does not show that independent
parts worked; they did not have their own bodies.

## Changes

- Recipes now declare physical object ownership before mesh batching. The
  100 recipes produce 152 independently registered rigid bodies. A suitcase
  can fall while its larger carousel remains; a nearby blue rack plate can
  fall while the far plate and base remain. Connected structural parts stay
  together. Loose food, parcels, tools, planters and stacked objects receive
  their own bodies, footprints and scoring receipts.
- Removing a support releases its carried objects to gravity. Their scripted
  motion stops individually. Building interiors keep their shell relationship
  and release through actual building fracture.
- Full XYZ extents and degree rotations now match the authoring contract.
  Ellipsoids retain all three axes instead of using only a radius and height.
- A startup spatial index corrects overlapping outdoor placements and keeps
  fixtures clear of traffic corridors. Interior assemblies are fitted inside
  their shells. The current city repairs 69 recipe placements and contains
  656 initial registered bodies; the food map retains 1,096.

## What the QA actually runs

`fidelity/scene_audit.gd` checks physical ownership, authored versus measured
mesh bounds, collision extents, footprint gates, registration and support
links. A separate physics-server shape query detects outdoor objects spawned
inside other scenery; it does not trust the placement algorithm's own index.

`tests/scene_qa_acceptance.gd` loads the production maps and moves the opening
to actual object positions. It uses shipped level radii, the normal food scan,
real ground contacts and gravity. It never directly calls `begin_swallow`,
disables a collider or assigns a downward velocity. Every one of the 152
recipe objects must produce an individual collection callback after its whole
body crosses the collection plane. Eight building shells also release their
23 protected recipe objects, and all 16 imported NPC variants are exercised.
The test records positions, radius, elapsed fall time and stuck-body state.

Four negative controls intentionally reintroduce defects: shared ownership,
doubled sphere size, missing swallowing registration, and an object spawned
inside a solid building. The same audit rejects all four. The first traversal
also found nine physical stalls against existing vehicles, kiosks, bins and
building edges. Those findings remain in `first-pass-findings.json`; its early
test-runner stale-reference errors were fixed separately, not counted as game
defects. All nine physical cases pass after placement repair.

Run the repeatable QA and guarded local export from this directory:

```sh
python3 tools/release_web.py --godot .build/godot-standard/Godot.app/Contents/MacOS/Godot
```

This runs scene QA on source, exports into a temporary candidate directory,
runs the same QA against the exported PCK, and only then replaces `build/web`.
A failed QA/export preserves the previous preview. Logs, source hashes and
export checksums are retained in `artifacts/scene-qa/`. The suite also belongs
to the standard `tools/validate_mobile.py` runner as `--suite scene_qa`.

## Results and limits

Source and the final exported PCK each pass **756 scene checks**, with **152/152
individual recipe collection receipts**. Seven focused regression suites pass
156 additional checks, including rim contacts, no movement tether, NPC falls,
building destruction, traffic, both maps and food rain. Two additional full-round
suites pass, including 24 city checks. City contested/solo completion takes
93.53/106.23 simulation seconds; food contested/solo completion takes
105.48/87.67 seconds. These results are recorded separately in `rounds.json`.

Two 600-frame Mac headless traces measure city opening P95 **8.153 ms** and
grown-city P95 **9.391 ms**; worst frames are **9.262 ms** and **12.667 ms**.
These measure CPU simulation only, not browser/GPU/thermal performance or
iPhone 14 acceptance. The QA checks authored ownership contracts and physics;
it is not an image-based detector of every possible visual defect.
