## Acceptance criteria for the implementation

1. Menu, all four maps and the case scene have explicit, different musical
   identities. Renaming or pitch-shifting the same loop does not satisfy this.
2. Each borrowed recording has an author release, actual license declaration,
   pinned source/member hashes and a truthful preparation record. Originals
   identify the authored notes/synthesis and do not copy a reference melody.
3. Avoid piercing repeating upper-register bells, hard snare/hat transients,
   stacked lead lines, or an accumulation of effect tails. Record any remaining
   brightness/density risk honestly; low overall gain alone cannot prove a
   pleasant arrangement.
4. The tracks have comparable low background loudness and no clipping. Loops
   must preserve the intended phrase and not introduce conspicuous silence or
   a numerical boundary discontinuity. Signal checks are evidence, not an
   auditory verdict.
5. The case score stays subordinate to the reel/reveal. Its timing must support
   the real 4.25-second animation, and immediate skip/close must not leave a
   competing musical cue behind. A rare reward should change phrase/color,
   not blast the player with a louder crash.
6. A menu return restores the overall theme; entering the selected playable map
   selects that map's identity. Browsing cards should not produce abrupt musical
   fragments. Preferences, focus/suspension and pause remain respected.
7. Final review refers to the final files and runtime changes by exact evidence.
   Root's test results and this independent art assessment remain distinguishable.
