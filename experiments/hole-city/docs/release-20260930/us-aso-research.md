# United States ASO research — 30 September / 1 October 2026

## Recommended search targets

This is an intent-based recommendation for the first U.S. release, not a paid
traffic plan. Public U.S. App Store searches were observed on 30 September 2026.
They are website observations, not authenticated iPhone ranking measurements.
No search-volume, keyword-difficulty or conversion dataset was available.

| Priority | Exact phrase | Observed game results | Fit for Hole Feast |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `black hole games` | All in Hole, Hole.io, Attack Hole, Hole Em All, Eat All | The most direct genre intent: guide a hole, swallow objects, grow. |
| 2 | `hole games` | All in Hole, Hole.io, Hole Busters 3D, Hole Em All, Eat All | Strong genre intent, although it covers a broader range of hole mechanics. |
| 3 | `eat the world` | Hole.io, Tasty Planet Forever, Tasty Blue, Hole Master, Gobble | Fits the small-to-large eating fantasy and the city/food worlds. |
| Defer | `black hole` | Game results mixed with several video-saving/splitting utilities | Ambiguous search intent; do not prioritize solely because the phrase is short. |
| Defer | `eating games` | Mukbang/ASMR, cooking and food-runner games mixed with Hole.io | Food theme alone does not establish matching gameplay intent. |

Search order is not evidence of traffic size or a forecast for a newly released
app. A first-place result cannot be promised. Recheck relevance and actual
organic product-page conversion after release before revising priorities.

## Evidence and positioning

- [Hole.io](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hole-io/id1389111413) uses **Eat The World** as its subtitle. Its listing shows recognizable city objects, large holes and growth progression.
- [All in Hole: Black Hole Games](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/all-in-hole-black-hole-games/id6503284107) places the category phrase in its name. Its screenshots emphasize recognizable food piles and a clearly visible foreground hole.
- [Apple App Store search guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/) describes title, subtitle, keywords and category relevance alongside user behavior. Keywords have a 100-character limit; avoid duplicate words, irrelevant terms and competing app names.
- [Apple localization guidance](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/localize-app-information/) supports localized product metadata. This release uses English (U.S.) and U.S. availability; performance in another storefront must be evaluated separately.

Raw search observations and reference captures are retained locally in the ignored
`build/ios-release-20260930/market-research` directory. Competitor images are
research references only and are not included in our store artwork.

## Metadata decision

- Name: **Black Hole Feast: Eat & Grow** — 28 characters.
- Subtitle: **Swallow Cities & Tasty Treats** — 29 characters.
- Keywords: `food,world,collect,skins,physics,donut,cake,sushi,offline,relaxing,arcade` — 73 characters.

The title already carries black, hole, eat and grow. The keyword field adds world
and food-specific terms without repeating those words. It omits competing brand
names and unsupported puzzle, idle or multiplayer claims. The descriptive word
“game” in a target search phrase does not require stuffing “games” into the title
or keyword field; relevance and actual results still need post-release evidence.

The current copy accurately describes two maps, earned coins, original skins,
computer-controlled rivals, BOOST, collapsing objects, City Tram and Sushi
Express. It must not claim all competitor features. Online teams, chat,
tournaments, unlimited levels and production ads/IAP are not implemented.
