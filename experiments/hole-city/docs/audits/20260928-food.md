# Snack Garden reference audit — September 28, 2026

This audits the current v0.7.0 project against the user's requested dense,
appetizing food city, large independent collapses, and sustained food rain.
The target combines the user's Hole.io / Hole Em All direction with the food
city idea from Cloudy with a Chance of Meatballs. It does not claim that the
film or a reference game uses particular physics or timing values.

Evidence is separated below into executed headless simulation, source/plan
inspection, and presentation judgments. Browser control is unavailable. No
browser screenshot, human play session, sound playback, phone FPS, or final
Godot lighting assessment was performed. The tests use `--test`, never the
player's save. Runtime code and the running preview were not changed.

## Current facts that should not be reported as missing

The current map has 2,594 initial physical objects, 136 food buildings, 15
heaps, ten rain recipes, 48 citizens, seven neighborhoods, 15 road segments,
and 35 walking paths. It no longer has the previous nine-cell road grid.
The existing independent-tier/NPC fall acceptance is current; this audit did
not reproduce a blanket "NPCs cannot fall" or "all food is fused" defect.

Rounded mass geometry and a glossy ingredient shader are present in
`fidelity/food_city.gd:38`, `fidelity/food_surface.gd`, and
`fidelity/food_glaze.gdshader:10`. Saturated frosting colors are explicit at
`fidelity/food_shapes.gd:5`. Lack of rounded forms or highlights should not be
claimed from the older flat preview. Final phone appearance remains unreviewed.

## F1 — P1: Settled food is counted as airborne and blocks further rain

**Measured defect.** In a 75-second simulation with a stationary starting hole,
no collections and opponents disabled, 24 rain bodies had spawned by ten
seconds. All 24 were nearly stationary by fifteen seconds. Nevertheless, all
24 remained awake, and the spawned count stayed exactly 24 through 75 seconds.
The rain produced only four waves.

`fidelity/food_city.gd:301` classifies every non-sleeping, unfrozen rain body as
airborne. At `:303`, the 24-body airborne cap consequently makes the next wave
size zero. A rested object's sleep state is not evidence that it is still
falling. The swept landing correction at `fidelity/food_body.gd:25` also rewrites
position and vertical velocity at supported contacts; whether that is the
engine's exact reason for never sleeping needs a separate isolation test.

There is a second eventual stop: `food_city.gd:297` removes only freed bodies,
so landed uneaten food also fills the 96-live-body cap. No landed-food lifetime
or replenishment policy exists. This latter condition is source-derived; the
executed idle probe first hit the 24-awake-body failure instead.

**Impact:** the requested ongoing food shower can disappear after the opening
seconds even though the player sees settled food, not a sky full of food.

**Acceptance:** run stationary and traveling 120-second sessions without eating
rain food. New waves must still occur in the 30-, 60-, and 90-second windows;
actual falling bodies and retained landed food must have separate bounded
budgets. Replenishment must not visibly delete food beside the player. Retain
the existing ten-recipe landing and collection checks.

## F2 — P2: The round can finish before the city-scale destruction phase

**Measured pacing gap and result-state behavior.** The normal solo autoplay
route completed at 420 points in 45.67 simulated seconds, level eight, radius
7.2. Only three of 136 buildings were consumed; 133 remained entirely intact.
The scene initially offers 9,333 points, so the 420 target is only 4.5% of its
initial score supply. This is one automated route, not a prediction of human
completion speed.

`fidelity/game.gd:195` ends immediately at the score plus food-goal threshold.
The food goal at `:577` only requires ten small foods, four donuts and one cake;
it does not require a large heap or landmark collapse. The result transition
at `:543` freezes every remaining body immediately. In this run nine moving
foods were frozen at the transition; four were still 6.5–10.5 metres above
ground. This is a deliberate current end-state rule, not an active-round
floating-object reproduction.

**Impact:** building more districts does not ensure the player experiences
them. The reward moment can interrupt the food rain or collapse it is meant
to celebrate.

**Acceptance:** a normal, unmodified-score play path should demonstrate a large
heap and a distinctive food landmark collapsing before settlement. Choose an
explicit completion/bonus phase and allow already-triggered visual falls to
resolve before the result backdrop freezes. Award the round reward once.

## F3 — P2: The starting composition deliberately has a large building-free court

**Verified layout, presentation inference.**
`tools/build_food_neighborhoods.py:140` rejects buildings within 11 metres plus
their reserved radius of the spawn at `(1, 23)`. The nearest building center
is 13.15 metres away; its reserved envelope starts 11.40 metres away. There are
no building centers within ten metres of the start. The three starter heaps
at `fidelity/food_city.gd:73` help the first bite but do not establish a built
street around it.

The existing `artifacts/food-street-network/opening.png` illustrates the court
and food distribution using actual meshes. It is an approximate offline
render with missing characters, UI and contact shadows, not a game screenshot.
The perspective camera at `fidelity/game.gd:266` looks at this court initially.

**Impact:** the opening can still read as food on a broad mint/cream floor even
though the total map has 136 buildings. Increasing the global object count
alone will not change this first impression.

**Acceptance:** compose an opening view with a nearby identifiable food street,
two readable building frontages, an accessible starter cascade and a landmark
in depth. Check a real 375-point portrait capture with the HUD visible; preserve
unobstructed movement and starter-size edible access. Do not fill the court
indiscriminately or rely on whole-map density as the visual criterion.

## F4 — P2: Landmarks are taller copies of four building recipes

**Verified asset limitation, presentation inference.** The live scene contains
37 cake, 35 donut, 33 burger and 31 pancake buildings. All nine landmarks use
those same four recipes: two cake, three donut, one burger and three pancake.
`tools/build_food_neighborhoods.py:169` selects the four recipes; `:175` makes
landmarks by changing floor counts and a role label. `fidelity/food_city.gd:161`
stacks the same recipe at every floor, with small offsets and a berry/donut
topper. `fidelity/food_shapes.gd:96` supplies one shared square facade grammar.

**Impact:** the connected street plan is improved, but the skyline still reads
as repeated food towers. Distinctive places, street-level shops and unusual
large food silhouettes requested for a convincing food city are absent.

**Acceptance:** add a small set of clearly different silhouettes and uses,
such as a cake plaza shop, a burger diner and a spanning food landmark. Identify
each district from a gameplay-camera silhouette/contact sheet without text.
Keep edible parts and support chains independent; more colors or more floors
of the same recipe do not count as additional building families.

## F5 — P2: Collapse has physics but very little food-specific feedback

**Verified code gap; perceived feel is not audio/video-tested.**
`fidelity/food_body.gd:55` starts physical fall and records collapse. The record
at `fidelity/food_city.gd:218` is telemetry only. There is no presentation event
for a food landing impact, a sequence of collapsing tiers or a large heap
collapse. The collection path at `fidelity/game.gd:505` updates counters and
calls `ping(points)`. At `:585` that is one of three short synthesized sine
tones selected by points, shared across food types. The HUD has a food-goal
icon bounce (`fidelity/ui.gd:675`), but this is not a world-space collapse effect.

**Impact:** a correctly falling pancake, egg and cake can still feel similar.
The appetizing, tactile reward the user requested is underrepresented even
when the geometry, saturated colors and physical collection are correct.

**Acceptance:** demonstrate small landing, multi-tier fall and large collapse
with distinct, bounded impact/crumb/glaze and sound feedback at the actual
contact locations. Keep it readable beneath the hole and HUD, cap simultaneous
effects, and compare videos with sound on/off so effects supplement real falls.

## F6 — P3: Almost 40% of the road network has no vehicle route

**Verified route coverage gap.** `fidelity/food_city.gd:77` puts all eight sushi
vehicles on one `Neighborhoods.lane_sample` loop. The authored plan has 609.96
metres of roads, but the routed boulevard is 370.00 metres. Its five connected
branch streets total 239.96 metres (39.3%) and have no vehicle paths.
`tools/build_food_neighborhoods.py:89` constructs the traffic path only while
building the ten ring segments; branch roads at `:100` are excluded.

**Impact:** branch intersections and inner food streets can look lifeless
despite the correct, non-grid street plan. This is not a claim that current
vehicles drive off the road; existing containment tests cover the loop.

**Acceptance:** add at least one inner route with valid intersection handling,
show vehicles entering and leaving a branch in a sustained camera sample,
and retain footprint-clearance, eaten-car cleanup and pedestrian separation
checks. Do not add traffic merely as decoration outside the authored lanes.
